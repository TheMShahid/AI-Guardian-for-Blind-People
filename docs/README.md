## Reading order

1. PID-001 for project purpose and boundaries.
2. PMP-001 and GOV-001 for project governance and document control.
3. SRS-001 and RTM-001 for candidate requirements and traceability.
4. SYS-ARCH-001, ADR-REG-001, and ICD-001 for system structure, decisions, and interfaces.
5. SAF-001, SEC-001, ACC-001, DATA-001, and VVP-001 for assurance.
6. AIML-001 and HW-001 for AI and physical-device feasibility.
7. OPS-001 and USER-001 for future operations and user-facing communication.

## Document interpretation

These are engineering planning artifacts. A refined candidate requirement is not automatically approved, implemented, or verified. Open questions, TBD values, assumptions, and validation-pending states must be resolved through research, review, and evidence before release. Document lifecycle, requirement lifecycle, and verification-evidence lifecycle are separate and governed by GOV-001.
