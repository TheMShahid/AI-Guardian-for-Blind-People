## Reading order

1. MVP-001 for the proposed first workflow and technology evaluation.
2. UC-001 for actors, user journeys, use cases, and failure/recovery scenarios.
3. ENG-READY-001 for the consolidated engineering preparation status and implementation gates.
4. PID-001 for project purpose and boundaries.
5. PMP-001 and GOV-001 for project governance and document control.
6. SRS-001 and RTM-001 for candidate requirements and traceability.
7. SYS-ARCH-001, ADR-REG-001, ICD-001, and ICD-MVP-001 for system structure, decisions, and interfaces.
8. SAF-001, SEC-001, ACC-001, DATA-001, and VVP-001 for assurance.
9. AIML-001 and HW-001 for AI and physical-device feasibility.
10. OPS-001 and USER-001 for future operations and user-facing communication.

## Document interpretation

These are engineering planning artifacts. A refined candidate requirement is not automatically approved, implemented, or verified. Open questions, TBD values, assumptions, and validation-pending states must be resolved through research, review, and evidence before release. Document lifecycle, requirement lifecycle, and verification-evidence lifecycle are separate and governed by GOV-001. ENG-READY-001 records the current consolidated readiness assessment; its open gates must not be treated as completed evidence.
