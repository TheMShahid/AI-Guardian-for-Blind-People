## 1. Project intent
AI Guardian for Blind People is being designed as supplementary assistance for selected visual-information tasks. Future versions may support user-initiated text reading, object information, or scene descriptions through accessible audio interaction.

This repository currently documents a project in initiation/planning. It is not a finished or validated assistive device.

## 2. Intended interaction concept
A future workflow may be: open the accessible application/activate supported device; choose or speak a supported task; review permission/capture status; initiate capture; wait for processing; hear the result and uncertainty; request repetition, stop playback, or end the session. Exact controls will be designed and evaluated with users.

## 3. Important limitations
AI can misidentify objects, misread text, omit relevant details, or generate incorrect descriptions. Camera view, lighting, distance, motion, noise, connectivity, and model limitations affect results. Never rely on this proposed system for safe road crossing, guaranteed obstacle avoidance, or emergency decisions. Continue using established mobility aids and techniques.

## 4. Privacy expectations
A future product should make capture status clear, request permissions at point of use, minimize storage, and explain local/cloud processing. Research or training use requires separate informed authorization.

## 5. Publication note
Revise this guide before public prototype/release so it reflects tested workflows, supported devices/languages, permissions, and limitations.
