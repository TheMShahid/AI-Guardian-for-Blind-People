# Research Data and Ethics Plan

## 1. Purpose
Establish responsible practices for user research, dataset acquisition, annotation, model evaluation, and participant/bystander information.

## 2. Research principles
Respect autonomy, accessibility, dignity, informed consent, confidentiality, fair compensation, and withdrawal rights where applicable. Include blind and low-vision people in decisions affecting them. Avoid extractive practices.

## 3. Participant research
Before recruitment, define research questions, eligibility, accessible information sheets, consent, compensation, risks, data collection, retention, withdrawal, and contact details. Obtain institutional ethics review or required approvals before research that requires them.

## 4. Dataset inventory
For each asset record ID, description, source, owner, acquisition date, provenance, license, consent, permitted purposes, sensitive content, collection/annotation method, storage/access/retention/deletion, split/leakage controls, and limitations.

## 5. Data separation
Separate participant research data, operational user data, public benchmarks, and model-training data. Access is purpose-limited. Product consent does not imply research or training consent.

## 6. Annotation quality
Document label definitions, annotator guidance, quality checks, adjudication, and inter-annotator agreement where appropriate. Preserve uncertainty and ambiguous cases.

## 7. Bystander privacy
Images/audio may capture non-consenting people. Minimize capture, avoid unnecessary identification, restrict access, redact where feasible, and define deletion/retention. Do not infer sensitive personal attributes.

## 8. Security and publication
Use access controls, encryption, secure transfer, and approved storage. Never commit private recordings, credentials, or unlicensed datasets to a public repository. Release data only after rights, privacy, consent, and re-identification review.

## 9. Open work
Complete data inventory, ethics determination, consent materials, retention schedule, dataset licensing review, annotation protocol, and data-access policy before collecting/using participant data.
